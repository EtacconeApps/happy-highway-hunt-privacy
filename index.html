Happy Highway Hunt

Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026 Applies to iOS & Android
Happy Highway Hunt is a family road-trip game. This page explains, in plain terms, what information the app collects, why, and how it's handled. Short version: we collect only what's needed to sync a trip between the phones playing it together — the players' names, a random device ID, and the trip's own game data. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking.
1. Information we collect 2. How we use it 3. Sharing & third parties 4. Storage & security 5. Retention & your choices 6. Your rights 7. Children's privacy 8. Changes 9. Contact

1Information we collect

When you set up a family group and play a trip, the app stores the following in order to sync results across everyone's phones:

WhatWhat it isWhy we collect it
Player name The name you give a player profile (e.g. a first name or nickname). Shown on bets, trip history, and the "Wrapped" recap so results are attributed to the right person.
Device / user ID A random identifier created automatically when the app first opens (via anonymous sign-in) — not your Apple ID, Google account, email, or phone number. Ties your phone to your family group and player profile so trip data syncs correctly.
Gameplay data Bets placed, countries/plates spotted, timestamps, trip names, and results. This is the game itself — it's what gets synced and shown in Trip History and Wrapped.
🚫 We do not collect: your precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, browsing history, payment information, or any advertising identifier. The app doesn't use GPS — trip names and locations, if you add them, are typed in manually and optional.

2How we use it

Everything collected is used for exactly one purpose: making the game work across the phones playing together — placing and comparing bets, logging spotted plates in real time, keeping trip history, resuming a trip on a different phone if one runs out of battery, and building the yearly "Wrapped" recap. We don't use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to running the game.

3Sharing & third parties

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics networks. The only outside party involved is our cloud database provider, Google Firebase, which stores and syncs the data described above on our behalf, under its own security and data-processing terms. Firebase acts strictly as our infrastructure — it doesn't use your data for its own purposes.

We may disclose information if required to by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the app.

4Storage & security

Game data is stored in Google Firebase's cloud infrastructure and on your device (so the app keeps working without a signal). We rely on Firebase's access-control rules to keep one family's data separate from another's. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we don't collect anything beyond what's described above, which limits what could ever be exposed.

5Retention & your choices

  • Leaving a family stops your phone from syncing further, but trips already saved to that family's shared history aren't deleted, since other members still rely on them.
  • Clearing trip history (Settings → Data) permanently deletes the family's saved trip records from our servers. This doesn't affect player profiles or settings.
  • Disbanding a family (leaving as its last member) permanently retires that family's invite code and its data along with it.
  • If you'd like your data deleted outside of these in-app actions, contact us using the details below and we'll take care of it.

6Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these, email us at the address below. If you're in the European Economic Area, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

7Children's privacy

Happy Highway Hunt is designed to be played by families together and isn't directed at children under 13 as an independent user. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children without a parent or guardian setting up and supervising the family group. If you believe a child has provided us information outside of that context, contact us and we'll remove it.

8Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, post a notice inside the app (Settings → About). We encourage checking back here occasionally.
